Location: `after/platform/internal/observability/identity_cache_internal_test.go:21–24`.

The locally available pinned `client_golang@v1.24.1/prometheus/registry.go:475–490` explicitly launches `collectWorker` in a goroutine; completion accounting occurs after collector invocation. A failed `require` exits this worker with `FailNow`, before its normal completion accounting, potentially leaving Gather and the scrape stuck. This also directly violates the repository rule forbidding require in worker/handler goroutines. Return or capture deadline evidence from the callback and assert it in the test goroutine after scraping. Keep the collector callback free of fatal test assertions.
