## Finding

**[P1] Separate unmanaged fake/operator sessions from the managed retirement inventory.** `clock-control.md:79` requires fake.env to use the same owning `zns_app` role, `prerequisites.compose.yaml:107-113` runs fake outside the managed generation, and `replacement.json:7` inventories every `zns_app` session. The preparation sequence starts fake before coordinator (`clock-control.md:119-120`). At this exact commit, `platform/cmd/zns/runtime_lifecycle.go:22-32,44-51` treats fake as unmanaged and opens its ordinary database pool; `platform/internal/store/store.go:19-20,38-44` opens and pings that pool. Fake reads persistent state at startup (`platform/internal/sandbox/fake.go:67-78`) and keeps the pool until shutdown.

`platform/internal/replacement/postgres.go:35-38` collects every managed-role session in the database. `platform/internal/replacement/coordinator.go:126-134,142-160` rejects sessions outside the current generation's app/admit/media names, and retirement requires zero managed-role sessions. Consequently a live fake session either blocks the initial retirement barrier as unknown or, even if named deceptively as an allowed generation session, prevents zero-session retirement. Fake is not stopped by the coordinator's six-component retirement. The required preparation sequence cannot reliably start the managed application, and real replacement cannot preserve an active fake/UI as specified.

The same role-sharing issue affects ordinary registration-read/role-control tools during an active managed scenario: these use tool.env's owning `zns_app`, product-fixture is unmanaged, and a coordinator inventory taken during their connection can reject it and retire the runtime. Completing a short command between snapshots does not establish safety.

Resolve the actual lifecycle/role composition before readiness. Keep migrations, seed and genuine fixture initialization under the required owning nonsuperuser, while giving persistent fake and permitted live operator sessions an explicit isolation mechanism compatible with the retirement barrier. Do not conceal unmanaged connections with current-generation application names or remove the managed application role from inventory. If the intended solution requires a source change or a separately scoped fake/operator login, include that dependency and its scoped permissions in the reviewed composition. Verify startup with fake already running, live permitted role/read commands, and genuine coordinator replacement.
