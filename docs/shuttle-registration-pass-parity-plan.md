# Registration and pass workflow QA

Required workflows are profile display-name entry; required legal details through solo waiting-list registration; pair invitation and acceptance; administrator pass assignment; pending payment evidence, human review and top-up; and the complete chain through final participant readback.

1. Open registration and choose the event and role. Observe any missing-field expectation.
2. Save legal fields in the form without Loom. Return to a fresh role choice and register once.
3. Invite and accept through the supported pair interface. Retain original partner/role consent and verify current membership.
4. Have an authorized administrator prepare and explicitly confirm both pass assignments. Verify frozen prices, deposits and deadlines from each participant interface.
5. Submit deposit and top-up evidence. An authorized reviewer confirms or rejects the exact evidence; pending submissions do not increase the confirmed balance.
6. Read final registrations, pair and pass balances. Replay an operation or recover a lost response with its original key; no second pair, pass or payment may result.

Repeat the chain across both interface handoffs. Include a graceful restart while an expectation or approval is pending, stale/foreign choices, current authority loss and deadline boundaries. Legal details remain independent of the public display name. Preserve original receipts and failed observations separately from later results.
